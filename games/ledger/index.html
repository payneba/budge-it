            You have the federal checkbook

            1. Move the sliders to cut or grow each part of the budget, and flip switches for specific reforms.
            2. Raise or lower taxes under Taxes & revenue.
            3. Watch the households, voters and the economy react as you go.
            4. There are three ways to judge a plan, and they conflict. Electability: will voters re-elect you? Shared sacrifice: is the burden spread evenly? Hardship: how much does it hurt households overall? A move that helps one often hurts another.
            5. Get the deficit to zero. Then see how your plan grades out on all three.
